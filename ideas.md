# Portfolio redesign direction

## Theme: Field Notebook / Living Archive

An academic portfolio that feels like a field notebook opened on a research desk: tactile ivory paper, ink-black type, survey blue, and a restrained rust annotation color. The design should feel materially rich rather than minimalist, and should be clearly distinct from the reference Wix portfolio's pale-sage, centered, white-card treatment.

## Design movement

Editorial archive meets cartographic field journal. Preserve the existing information architecture and academic seriousness, but make the page feel authored: ruled dividers, index labels, marginal metadata, paper layers, stamped section numbers, and photographs treated as documented evidence.

## Core principles

- Preserve the existing page order, navigation, remote JSON content, and academic portfolio tone.
- Replace the original pale sage / centered Wix-like presentation with a darker ink-and-paper system and asymmetric editorial composition.
- Use visible material texture, offset panels, hairline rules, and dense metadata so the interface is not minimalist.
- Keep interaction calm, direct, keyboard accessible, and responsive.

## Color philosophy

- Paper: #f1eadc, warm ivory
- Paper bright: #fffaf0
- Ink: #202b2b
- Muted graphite: #6d756e
- Rule: #cfc2ad
- Rust annotation: #bb573d
- Survey blue: #4b7181
- Deep ink panel: #202b2b

## Layout paradigm

Keep the same major sections and dedicated pages, but introduce a persistent archival header, left-side section indexes, offset content sheets, wide image bands, and structured lists instead of generic centered cards. On inner pages, retain the original page content but give it a document-like reading surface.

## Signature elements

- Hero caption anchored low-right over the image, inspired by the supplied photo: a name lockup, thin rule, role line, and small metadata label.
- Section headings with a rust index number and uppercase mono eyebrow.
- Paper sheet surfaces with subtle grain, hard corners, inset rules, and offset shadows.
- Survey-blue links and rust hover lines.
- Metadata rendered in IBM Plex Mono.

## Typography system

- Display: Fraunces for name, hero and section titles.
- Interface/body: DM Sans for navigation, body copy, and controls.
- Metadata: IBM Plex Mono for labels, dates, counts, and small annotations.

## Brand essence and voice

A careful researcher who brings field observation, data, and public-minded scholarship together. Voice is precise, observant, and quietly personal; avoid generic marketing language.

## Wordmark / signature color

Use the person's name as an editorial wordmark with a rust square marker. Rust is the signature annotation color; survey blue is reserved for links and research references.
